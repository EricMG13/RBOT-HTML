## Findings

### Bottom line

Refinancing is the binding credit risk: USD 646 million of debt matures within 24 months against USD 310 million of cash, and no committed refinancing is in place.
Basis: NBG_2025-03-31_annual-report.pdf, pages 43–45; calculation R1

### Key findings

- The funding need inside two years is USD 336 million after cash, so the issuer must reach the market or extend the revolver before the 2026 maturity.
Basis: calculation R1
- A liability-management exercise is possible but not signalled: the documents read state no exchange offer, and the facility terms that would permit one were not supplied.
Basis: NBG_2025-03-31_annual-report.pdf, page 44

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The consistent earnings record supports market access. A signed extension of the revolver would close most of the funding need.
Basis: Fundamental credit (CP-2)

### Limitations

- The facility agreement was not read, so the room for a priming or drop-down transaction is not assessed.
